What O Canada Cars does

We score every vehicle sold in Canada out of 100 based on where it was actually built and where its major parts came from. Final assembly location, North American content percentage, engine origin, transmission origin — all pulled from public government data and combined into one transparent number.

No agenda. No guessing. You can read the full methodology on the site. The data sources are cited. The formula is published.

How the score breaks down

75+
Strong Canadian connection Typically assembled in Canada — Oshawa, Cambridge, Alliston, Windsor. High North American parts content.
50–74
Mixed Assembled in the US or Mexico under CUSMA, with meaningful North American supply chain involvement.
0–49
Limited Canadian connection Primarily overseas assembly and parts. Low North American content.

Once you're on the site, there's an option to exclude US-assembled vehicles from your results entirely if that's where you're at right now. It's a toggle — your call, not the default.
