Compare origins. Choose with context.

O Canada Cars publishes model-year vehicle records and an origin score when the available evidence supports one. U.S.-filing configurations may be labelled Canadian availability unverified or identified as not representing a Canadian-market version.

What the O Canada Score measures

The score compares four manufacturing-origin categories for a model-year record. It is not a measure of Canadian-only parts, jobs supported, vehicle quality, or a specific vehicle’s origin.

Final assemblyup to 50 points
Combined U.S./Canadian parts contentup to 30 points
Engine or electric-motor originup to 10 points
Transmission originup to 10 points

Important: AALA reports combine U.S. and Canadian parts content; they do not identify the Canadian share. The method, weights, and record-level source notes are published, while source coverage and verification vary by record. Scores are withheld when evidence does not support a precise result.

Check availability and the individual vehicle

Records describe supported model years and configurations. They do not confirm that every listed configuration is sold in Canada or identify the origin of a specific vehicle. Read the availability label, then confirm the vehicle’s assembly location with its VIN, the manufacturer, or the dealer.

If you choose to avoid U.S.-assembled vehicles, the main site lets you filter them out. The O Canada Score and that filter do not determine tariff exposure or eligibility under CUSMA.

Use the information for your own decision

If supporting Canadian manufacturing matters to you, compare the published origin information and its limits. The score does not estimate the jobs or economic impact associated with an individual purchase.

O Canada Cars is an independent information resource. Verify details that matter to your purchase directly with the manufacturer or dealer.
